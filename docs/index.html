Know where your money goes.

AuraSpend is a personal expense tracker for Android. Plan budgets, track subscriptions and savings goals, and let bank messages be classified on your phone, not on a server.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

The features below are all in the app today, in every build.

Bank SMS, sorted for you

Turn on auto-categorise and AuraSpend reads new bank messages from your inbox and proposes a transaction. Swipe to save or dismiss. Off by default.

Budgets per category

Set a limit for each category and watch progress against it on the home screen and in Plan.

Subscriptions

Track recurring payments in one list, with renewal dates and what they cost you each month.

Insights

Cash flow over the week and spending by category, so you can see where the money actually goes.

Savings goals

Give a goal a target and a date, then add to it as you go.

CSV in and out

Export every transaction to a CSV file, or import one. Your data is never locked into the app.

Drive backup & restore

Keep a copy in your own Google Drive and bring it back in one tap. Nothing is uploaded until you ask.

Review first

Nothing is saved without your say.

Bank messages that AuraSpend can read appear in a review queue. Save the ones that are right, dismiss the rest, and unreadable messages are kept for you to look at rather than dropped.

  • Sender-aware parsing. Bank senders are recognised and parsed by bank-specific rules.
  • Unrecognised messages are kept. If a message carries an amount but no parser claims it, it goes to a separate list.
  • Optional smart categories. A 22 MB on-device model can improve categories. The app works fully without it.
Plan

Budgets and goals in one place.

Budgets show how much of each limit you have used. Savings goals and subscriptions sit alongside them on the Plan tab.

  • Per-category monthly budgets with progress rings.
  • Savings goals with a target amount and optional date.
  • Recurring subscriptions with renewal tracking.
Your look

Light, dark, or true black.

Choose System, Light, Dark or AMOLED black. On Android 12 and later you can match your wallpaper with dynamic colour. Amounts are formatted for your locale — or pick one of 30 currencies in onboarding or Settings.

  • Interface in English and Hindi.
  • Currency: your device's default, or one of 30 to choose from.
  • Built with Material 3 and the Aurora design system.
Privacy

Your money data stays on your phone — unless you ask it to leave.

AuraSpend has no accounts and no servers that hold your data. Every build contains the same code, and none of them include an analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK.

On-device by defaultTransactions live in a local database. Classification runs on your phone.
Network only when you askThe model download, and Google Drive backup and restore, are the only network features. All are optional.
No trackingNo analytics, advertising identifiers or telemetry in any build.
