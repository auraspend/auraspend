## What was added

Four marketing posters were designed using Canva and exported to `docs/marketing/reddit/` at **1080×1350 px (4:5 portrait)** for Reddit launch promotion following the move from closed testing to production on Google Play:

1. **`01-auraspend-launch-reddit.png`**: Google Play launch announcement highlighting automated SMS/UPI tracking, on-device privacy, and open-source availability.
2. **`02-auraspend-privacy-manifesto.png`**: Comparison graphic contrasting AuraSpend's zero-cloud, 100% on-device architecture against traditional trackers requiring net banking logins.
3. **`03-auraspend-upi-daily-expenses.png`**: Everyday automation hook targeting Indian UPI spenders ("Stop typing every ₹20 chai").
4. **`04-auraspend-feature-overview.png`**: 4-quadrant feature breakdown (Auto SMS, Budget Rings, Subscriptions, SQLCipher Encryption).

Each design is also accessible and editable via Canva links recorded in `walkthrough.md`.
