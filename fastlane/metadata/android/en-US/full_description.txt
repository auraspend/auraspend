Turn your bank's SMS into organised spending — entirely on your phone.

AuraSpend reads your bank and payment messages locally and turns them into
transactions, budgets, subscriptions and category insights. No accounts, no
servers, no analytics, no ads: your messages never leave the device.

• Automatic categorisation: 50+ per-bank parsers, refined by an optional
  on-device encoder (~22 MB MiniLM, downloaded only after an explicit consent
  tap; SHA-256 verified). The app works without it.
• Budgets, recurring-subscription detection, savings goals, CSV export and
  import.
• English and Hindi, Material 3 with light, dark and AMOLED themes.

This build ships without Google Play Services: Google Drive backup is not
available here. CSV export/import moves your data in and out.
