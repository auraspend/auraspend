What's new in 0.1.6

• Fewer wrong entries: reward-point, offer and fund notices are no longer saved as transactions.
• UPI/NEFT payments keep their real category instead of "Transfer".
• Money received from a person is income; money sent is an expense.
• SIP and mutual-fund debits are filed under Investments.
• Payee names show up again when the bank writes "Refno" without a space.
