What's new in 0.1.6

• A fresh terracotta look with softer shapes and new type.
• Smarter SMS classification: fewer wrong entries, better merchant names, and HDFC "if not you" debits are no longer dropped.
• Own-account transfers no longer count as spending; batched debit/credit messages book the primary movement.
• SIP and mutual-fund debits are investments, and corrections now teach future messages.
• Broader SMS scanning, with a safety guard for foreign-currency messages.
