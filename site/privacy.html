Privacy Policy

What the app keeps, what it can send over the network, and what you can do about both.

Effective 19 October 2026 · Applies to the Google Play build, the F-Droid build and to APKs built from the published source.

The short version

  • Your transactions, budgets and bank messages stay in an encrypted database on your phone.
  • AuraSpend has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. We run no servers that receive your data.
  • The app uses the network in two optional cases: downloading the categorisation model, and Google Drive backup or restore. Nothing is sent to us in either case.
  • Reading bank SMS is opt-in and off by default.
  • Phone numbers, UPI references and similar identifiers are masked out of your messages before they are stored.

1. What we collect

We collect nothing. AuraSpend has no sign-up, and the developers receive no data from the app.

The app stores the following on your device, and only there:

  • Transactions: amounts, dates, notes, categories and the type (income or expense), whether you entered them or the app created them from a bank message.
  • Budgets, subscriptions and savings goals that you create.
  • Categories you create or edit.
  • Bank messages the app has read, held in a local queue together with the sender and the message text, so that each one is processed once.
  • Preferences: theme, and whether automatic SMS categorisation and smart categories are on. Currency follows your device's locale unless you pick one.
  • Classification memory: the corrections you make, so the app remembers how you file a merchant.

AuraSpend does not contain an analytics SDK, an advertising SDK, a crash-reporting library, or any telemetry, in any build. The app makes no network request for usage data or diagnostics.

2. Bank SMS

If you turn on Auto-categorize messages in Settings, AuraSpend asks Android for permission to read SMS (READ_SMS). Only then does it read your inbox, and only for messages it can take as bank or card activity.

  • Processing happens on your device. Message text is never uploaded.
  • Messages that look like one-time passwords or alerts are skipped unless they also describe a movement of money. Messages that contain an amount but that no parser recognises are kept in a separate list for you to review, rather than discarded.
  • Identifiers are masked before storage. Phone numbers, UPI references, UTRs, folio ids and hash-like tokens are removed from the message text before it is written to the database, and from the notes and exports as well. Amounts, dates and the bank's own card mask are left intact so that the app still works. A one-off backfill masked the messages stored before this behaviour existed.
  • You can turn the feature off at any time. Turning it off stops new messages being read. It does not delete the transactions it has already created.
  • You can deny the permission and use AuraSpend fully. Expenses can be entered by hand, or imported from a CSV file.

Android's Auto Backup is configured to exclude the app's database, its SMS queue, its preferences and the model directory, so these are not copied to Google's backup service, and they are not carried over in a device-to-device transfer.

3. Network use

AuraSpend requests the Internet permission for two optional features. Those are the only times the app connects to anything.

ConnectionWhenWhat is sent
huggingface.coOnly when you tap to download the smart-categories model (about 22 MB).A request for the model file and its tokenizer. The request carries no account or message content.
Google (Drive API and sign-in)Only when you back up to, or restore from, Google Drive.Your Google sign-in and the backup file you asked to write or read. See section 4.

Nothing else is contacted. The app has no server of its own.

4. Google Drive

Google Drive is used for two things, and only when you ask: backing up your data to your own Drive, and restoring a backup. Both live under Settings → Data; restore is also offered during onboarding. This feature is not present in the F-Droid build — see section 8.

  • Scope: the app requests the drive.file scope. This lets it read and write only the files it creates, not the rest of your Drive.
  • Where it lives: the backup is a file named auraspend_backup.json in your own Google Drive.
  • Contents: transactions, categories, budgets, subscriptions, savings goals, the stored bank-message queue, the messages the app could not turn into a transaction, and the classification memory. Message bodies are stored with phone numbers, account fragments and reference ids masked, but they are still message text. The file is not encrypted by AuraSpend, so anyone who can open your Drive can read it.
  • Restore replaces data: restoring replaces the data on the device in a single transaction. If the restore fails, the existing data is left as it was.
  • Authentication: sign-in is handled by Google. We never see your Google password.

A backup runs only when you tap it, and it overwrites the previous backup file. There is no scheduled or background sync, and nothing is uploaded on its own.

Google's handling of data in your Drive is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. On-device model

The smart-categories model is a sentence encoder, about 22 MB, used to suggest categories. It is downloaded from Hugging Face only after you tap the download button, and it runs entirely on your phone. AuraSpend is fully functional without it — the parsing layer does the categorising on its own.

  • The file is checked against a fixed SHA-256 digest before it is used. A file that does not match is rejected.
  • You can delete the model in Settings. The app keeps working, and your transactions are not affected.

Hugging Face's handling of requests is governed by its privacy policy.

6. Storage and deletion

Your data is stored on this device, and kept until you remove it.

  • Individual records: delete a transaction, budget, subscription, goal or category from its screen.
  • Everything at once, from inside the app: Settings → Data → Delete all data wipes every table, re-seeds the default categories and forgets the position of the SMS scan, so a fresh scan can pick your inbox up again. It asks for confirmation first.
  • Everything at once, from outside the app: uninstall AuraSpend. Android removes its local data with the app. Clearing the app's storage in Android's Settings does the same without uninstalling. The encrypted database and its write-ahead log go with it.
  • Bank-message queue: processed messages remain in the local queue. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Unrecognised messages: deleted automatically after 30 days, by the same background pass that classifies new messages.
  • Smart-categories model: delete it from Settings.
  • Drive backups: uninstalling does not delete backups in your Google Drive. Delete the auraspend_backup.json file in Drive to remove it.

7. Security

  • The database is encrypted at rest. AuraSpend uses SQLCipher, and the passphrase is derived from a non-exportable key held in the Android Keystore. The passphrase is never written to disk, to a log, or into a backup. There is no fallback to plain text: if the key cannot be obtained, the app refuses to open the database rather than opening it unencrypted.
  • Identifiers are masked before storage, as described in section 2.
  • An optional app lock — a four-digit PIN stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash, with a biometric shortcut where the hardware supports it. It locks on a cold start and again after a minute in the background. There is deliberately no back door: “forgot PIN” wipes the data through the same path as “Delete all data”.
  • Your data is kept in an on-device database that other apps cannot read.
  • The app's release builds are signed, and the release process verifies the signatures before publishing. Each release publishes a SHA-256 checksum file alongside the artifacts.
  • Android's Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer exclude the database, the SMS queue, the preferences and the model directory.

No software is perfectly secure. Your phone's lock screen and device encryption are the main protection for data on a lost or shared device. Drive backups are not encrypted by AuraSpend; treat them as you would any file in your Drive. CSV exports are likewise plain files that you choose where to put them.

8. The three builds

AuraSpend ships in three forms. They contain the same application code; the differences are the packaging and the optional Google services.

BuildGoogle Drive backupIn-app update check
Google Play (prod)YesYes — a daily check with Play, and Settings → About → Check for updates
GitHub Releases APK (prod)YesYes
F-Droid (fdroid)No — F-Droid's inclusion policy excludes Google Play ServicesNo — F-Droid handles updates

The update check asks the Play Store app already on your device whether a newer version exists. Nothing about the question — or the answer — is sent to AuraSpend, which has no server to send it to; the record of which version you run stays between Android and Google Play, exactly as it does for every Play-installed app.

There is no analytics, advertising or telemetry in any of them, and no feature is held back behind a purchase. The differences above are recorded in the project's decision records, ADR 0012 and ADR 0013.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, most requests are answered by the app itself.

  • Access and portability: export all transactions to a CSV file from Settings → Data.
  • Correction: edit any transaction, budget, subscription, goal or category.
  • Erasure: see section 6.
  • Withdrawing consent: turn off automatic SMS categorisation, delete the model, or stop using Drive backup and restore. The Google permission can be revoked in your Google Account settings.

If you are in the EEA, UK, California or another region with similar rights, these rights apply to you. If you have a question the app cannot answer, use the contact details in section 12. Because we do not hold your data, we are generally unable to access, change or delete it for you, but we will tell you that and point you to the right place.

10. Children

AuraSpend is a personal finance tool. It does not collect information from anyone, including children. Parents and guardians should supervise the use of any finance app by a minor.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page is updated and the effective date at the top moves. The change is recorded in the project's public history at github.com/auraspend/auraspend, so earlier versions can be compared.

The 19 October 2026 revision added the encrypted-database, app-lock and identifier-masking facts to section 7, the identifier-masking fact to section 2, the in-app “Delete all data” action to section 6 — the previous version said the app had no such button, which was wrong — and a new section 8 describing the three builds.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, email awbuilds.support@gmail.com. For security issues, see SECURITY.md. We aim to reply within 30 days.
