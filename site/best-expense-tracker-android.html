The best offline expense tracker for Android — no account, no cloud

“Offline” is the most overused word in app descriptions. This page treats it as a testable claim: switch the network off, use the app for a week, see what breaks. Six trackers, one question.

Last updated 6 November 2026. Prices and features change; check the store listing before you install.

What “offline” should mean

There are three different things apps call offline, and only one of them is useful if privacy is the reason you are here.

  • Works without a connection, but syncs when it has one. The app is usable on a plane, then uploads everything later. Your data still lives on a server.
  • Works without a connection and never syncs. Data is on the device and nowhere else. Backups are files you move yourself.
  • Cache only. The shell opens offline; the actual features need the network. This is the one worth ruling out immediately.

If you want your spending to stay on your phone, you want the second kind. That is the column this comparison sorts on.

Pick AuraSpend if…

The reason you want offline is that you do not want a finance app holding your transaction history. AuraSpend has no server, no account and no analytics, and it does not ask you to trust a privacy policy to prove it — the source is public, including the bank parsers and the database layer. The database is encrypted with SQLCipher, the key lives in the Android Keystore and never touches disk, and there is no plain-text fallback if the key is unavailable.

It also does the thing most offline trackers cannot: it fills itself in. Bank and UPI messages are parsed on the device, so you are not typing every coffee. That combination — automatic capture plus zero network — is the actual reason this app exists.

Pick something else if…

  • You want automatic sync across devices. No server means no background sync. AuraSpend moves data by writing a JSON backup to your own Drive and restoring it elsewhere. If that is too manual, use a cloud app and accept the server.
  • You want bank or card feeds. AuraSpend never links an account. Wallet and Spendee do, in the countries where their aggregation partners operate.
  • You need multi-account with transfers. Money Manager is the stronger ledger here; AuraSpend keeps a single ledger, and multi-account is on its roadmap.
  • You want to see investments next to spending. None of the purely offline options do this well. That is a different category of app.
  • You are on iOS. AuraSpend is Android 11+ only.
  • You will not grant SMS access. Without it, AuraSpend becomes a manual tracker with CSV import. It still works; it stops being automatic.

The cost of staying offline

An offline app cannot recover your data for you. If the phone is lost and there is no backup, the ledger is gone — that is the direct consequence of the design, not an oversight. AuraSpend mitigates it with two export paths (CSV and a Drive snapshot) and by excluding the database from Android's automatic cloud backup, so a backup is something you choose rather than something that happens silently.

The second cost is speed of new features. A server-side app can fix a category or add a bank without shipping a release. AuraSpend ships bank parsers in the APK, which is why it has 53 of them and a review queue for everything it cannot read.

Questions people ask

What is the best offline expense tracker for Android?

AuraSpend is the only one here that is fully offline, free, ad-free, account-free and open source. Money Manager and Monefy are also genuinely offline, but both show ads on the free tier. Wallet, Goodbudget and Spendee depend on an account or a server for at least part of what they do.

Do offline expense trackers still back up my data?

They can, but the backup should be something you start. AuraSpend exports CSV and can write a single JSON snapshot to your own Google Drive — a folder you control, not the developer. An on-device app cannot recover your data if you lose the phone without a backup.

Is there an expense tracker that works with no internet connection at all?

Yes. AuraSpend parses bank SMS, categorises, budgets and reports entirely on the device; day-to-day use needs no connection. The only optional network calls are a one-time 22 MB model download you consent to, and a Drive backup you start yourself.

Can an offline expense tracker sync between phones?

Only by moving a file. Apps with no server cannot sync in the background. AuraSpend writes a JSON backup to your Drive and restores it on another phone; Money Manager and Monefy export files in the same way. Automatic multi-device sync requires a cloud app.

Is AuraSpend really free, or is there a paid tier?

Free, with no paid tier. No ads, no in-app purchases, no features held back. The source is published under the AGPL-3.0, so the absence of a paywall is checkable rather than promised.

Does AuraSpend work without Google Play Services?

Yes. There is a build with no Play Services dependency at all — no Drive backup, no in-app update check, CSV in and out. Everything else is identical. The download page lists both builds.
