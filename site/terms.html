2. The app

AuraSpend helps you record and review your personal spending. Its features include:

  • Manual entry of income and expenses.
  • Budgets per category, over a weekly, monthly or yearly period.
  • Subscriptions, savings goals, and recurring payments detected from your messages.
  • Optional reading of bank SMS, with a review step before anything is saved.
  • Insights and charts of your cash flow and spending.
  • CSV export and import.
  • Optional backup to and restore from your own Google Drive.
  • An optional app lock, and an encrypted database on the device.

The App runs on your device and has no service of its own that holds your data. We may change or remove features in later releases. Where a change affects data you have stored, we will say so in the release notes.
