How it works

Three steps. The third one is you.

AuraSpend never asks for your card number, your net banking credentials or an account with anyone. It reads the SMS your bank already sends, on your phone.

No scraping, no linking

AuraSpend never connects to your bank. It reads the same message you already read, with read-only access to your inbox. It cannot send an SMS and never asks for banking credentials.

54 parsers, and a fallback

The sender ID picks the parser that knows that bank's format — 54 of them, covering banks, cards, wallets and payment apps. Anything else falls back to a general engine.

Nothing written yet

A parsed transaction waits in a review queue. It reaches your ledger only when you swipe it there, and you can undo that in one tap.

Review first

Nothing is saved without your say.

Most money apps guess and move on. AuraSpend shows you exactly what it read, and waits.

  • Messages it cannot read are kept. If a message carries an amount and a movement verb but no parser claims it, it goes to a review list with its raw text — never into the bin.
  • Skipped messages stay visible. Every message it skips is listed in Smart Add, with a “Classify again” button, so a parser fix reaches back and re-queues it.
  • The model cannot delete your money. A transaction is discarded only when the parser found no amount and the on-device model is certain it is not one. The shipped encoder has never done that to a real debit.

The app

Everything you need, and none of the noise.

Every feature below ships in every build. There is no paywall, no premium tier and no in-app purchase.

Activity

Find anything, fast.

Every transaction you have kept, in one scrollable ledger — grouped by day, with the net amount for each day in the header.

  • Search by merchant or note, always visible at the top.
  • Filter by direction and by category, with each category in its own colour.
  • Step through months, and switch between newest and oldest first.
  • Swipe a row away to delete it, with undo.
Plan

Budgets, subscriptions and goals, in one place.

A budget for every category you care about, and a single screen that says plainly whether you are over.

  • Per-category budgets, shown as bars you can compare by length, with the real percentage — “122 % used”, never a clamped 100 beside a larger number.
  • Savings goals with a target amount and an optional date.
  • Subscriptions you track, plus the recurring payments AuraSpend has spotted in your messages — offered to you, never added silently.
  • The count on Plan is the count on the screen it links to.
Insights

See where it actually went.

A ranked answer to “where did the money go”, and how this month compares with the last one.

  • Spending by category, ranked, with the tail summarised rather than hidden.
  • Daily spending across the period you chose — this month, the last 30 days, or all time.
  • Your top merchants, with a count of how many times you spent there.
  • Tap any category or merchant to jump straight to the transactions behind it.
Quick Add

An expense in seconds.

For the things that never arrive by SMS — cash, a card paid in person, a shared bill.

  • A keypad with haptics, a category row, and merchant and date on one line.
  • The sheet opens expanded, so Save is never below the fold.
  • Smart Add and Manual are one tap away, not buried in a menu.
Your data

Yours to take out, and yours to keep.

You should be able to leave whenever you want, with everything intact.

CSV in and out

Export every transaction to a CSV file, or import one back. Re-importing is duplicate-checked, so a file you bring in does not double your history.

Drive backup

Keep a copy of your ledger in your own Google Drive and restore it on another phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you start a backup yourself.

App lock

A four-digit PIN, and optionally your fingerprint or face. The PIN is stored as a salted hash, never in plain text — and there is no back door.

Privacy and security

Your money data stays on your phone — unless you ask it to leave.

AuraSpend has no account, no server that holds your data, and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK in any build.

Everything AuraSpend can send over the network, and exactly when.
On the device Off the device
Transactions, categories, budgets, goals and subscriptions A Drive backup you start — one JSON file, in your own Drive
Your bank messages, and the queue of what it read The optional 22 MB categoriser — a file request that carries no message content
The categorisation model and its vocabulary, once downloaded Nothing else. The app has no server of its own.

Encrypted at rest

Your database is encrypted with SQLCipher, using a key derived from a non-exportable key in the Android Keystore. There is no fallback to plain text — the app refuses to open rather than open in the clear.

Scrubbed before it is stored

Phone numbers, UPI references, UTRs, folio ids and hash-like tokens are masked out of the message queue, the notes and the exports — while the amount, the date and the bank's own card mask survive, so the app still works.

Android's own backup is off

The database, the preferences and the model directory are excluded from both cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. A backup is something you start, not something that happens to you.

Questions

The short answers.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no AuraSpend account, no sign-in and no server. Install it, grant read access to your SMS if you want automatic tracking, and it works.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. It never asks for a card number, a customer ID, a net banking password or an OTP. It reads the same SMS you already read, and it cannot send an SMS.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Parsing, categorisation, budgets, goals and insights all run on the device. Day-to-day use needs no connection at all.

Is it really free?

Yes — no ads, no trackers, no accounts, no paywall and no in-app purchase. Every feature is in every build, and the source is open under the AGPL-3.0.
